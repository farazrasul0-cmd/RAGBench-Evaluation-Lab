Climate change is accelerating the melting of Arctic sea ice. Scientists have observed a dramatic reduction in Arctic ice extent over the past four decades. The primary driver is the accumulation of greenhouse gases, particularly carbon dioxide and methane, which trap heat in the atmosphere. The Arctic is warming at approximately four times the global average rate, a phenomenon known as Arctic Amplification. As ice melts, darker ocean water absorbs more solar energy, creating a positive feedback loop that further accelerates warming.
