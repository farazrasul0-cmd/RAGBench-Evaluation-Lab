Rising sea levels threaten coastal communities worldwide. Thermal expansion of warming ocean water and melting glaciers and ice sheets are the two main contributors to sea level rise. Current projections indicate that global mean sea level could rise by 0.3 to 1.0 meters by 2100 under moderate emission scenarios. Low-lying island nations such as Tuvalu and the Maldives face existential threats, with regular tidal flooding already disrupting settlements.
