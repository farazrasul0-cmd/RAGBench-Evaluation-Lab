Immune checkpoint inhibition targeting the PD-1/PD-L1 axis has revolutionized the therapeutic landscape of non-small-cell lung cancer (NSCLC). Nivolumab and pembrolizumab demonstrate durable objective response rates ranging from 15% to 20% in unselected patient populations, with significantly enhanced efficacy in patients with high PD-L1 expression (TPS >= 50%). Tumor mutation burden (TMB) serves as an independent predictive biomarker of response.
