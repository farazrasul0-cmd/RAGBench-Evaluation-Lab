Cytosine and adenine base editors enable programmable nucleotide transitions without requiring double-stranded DNA breaks. Genome-wide off-target analysis utilizing GUIDE-seq and high-throughput sequencing demonstrates that engineered Cas9 variants with reduced electrostatic interactions with non-target strands substantially attenuate guide-dependent off-target editing. Adenine base editors consistently display lower genome-wide off-target RNA deamination than cytidine deaminase variants.
