Chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy targeting CD19 achieves complete remission rates exceeding 80% in relapsed or refractory acute lymphoblastic leukemia. However, therapeutic durability is limited by antigen escape through CD19 loss or splice site mutations, alongside severe systemic toxicities including cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS), typically managed with tocilizumab and corticosteroids.
