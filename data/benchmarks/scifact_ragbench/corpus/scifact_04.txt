Sotorasib and adagrasib are small-molecule covalent inhibitors that selectively bind the switch-II pocket of the inactive GDP-bound state of KRAS G12C, trapping the oncoprotein in its inactive conformation. Phase II clinical trials demonstrate confirmed objective responses in approximately 37% to 43% of pretreated patients harboring KRAS G12C mutated non-small-cell lung adenocarcinoma. Secondary resistance mechanisms commonly involve secondary mutations in KRAS or bypass pathway activation.
