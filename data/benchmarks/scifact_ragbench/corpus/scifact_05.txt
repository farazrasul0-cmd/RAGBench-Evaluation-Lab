Nucleoside-modified mRNA encapsulated in ionizable lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) elicits robust antigen-specific neutralizing antibody titers and polyfunctional CD4+ and CD8+ T-cell responses. Incorporation of N1-methylpseudouridine prevents recognition by Toll-like receptors 7 and 8, mitigating innate immune activation and augmenting intracellular translation efficiency. LNP formulation with four lipid components ensures efficient cellular uptake and endosomal release.
