The composition of the intestinal microbiome significantly modulates host systemic immune responses and antitumor immunity during immune checkpoint inhibitor treatment. Metagenomic shotgun sequencing reveals that elevated abundance of Bifidobacterium longum, Akkermansia muciniphila, and Faecalibacterium species correlates with clinical benefit, enhanced CD8+ tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes, and improved progression-free survival in metastatic melanoma patients.
