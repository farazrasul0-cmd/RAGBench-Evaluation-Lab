Single-cell RNA sequencing reveals profound cellular heterogeneity across the tumor microenvironment (TME). Dissecting immune, stromal, and malignant cell populations unveils exhausted CD8+ T-cell subsets characterized by co-expression of LAG3, TIM3, and TIGIT alongside PD-1. Cancer-associated fibroblasts (CAFs) segregate into inflammatory CAFs (iCAFs) and myofibroblastic CAFs (myCAFs) with divergent roles in extracellular matrix remodeling and immunosuppression.
