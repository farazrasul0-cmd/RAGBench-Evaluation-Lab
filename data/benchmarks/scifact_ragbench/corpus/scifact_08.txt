Telomere shortening serves as a fundamental cell division counter, culminating in replicative senescence or apoptosis upon reaching the critical Hayflick limit. Upwards of 85% to 90% of human carcinomas bypass replicative senescence through transcriptional reactivation of the catalytic subunit of human telomerase reverse transcriptase (hTERT), commonly driven by recurrent somatic promoter mutations at positions -124 bp and -146 bp.
