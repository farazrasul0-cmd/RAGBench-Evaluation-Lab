Histone H3 lysine 27 trimethylation (H3K27me3), catalyzed by enhancer of zeste homolog 2 (EZH2) within the polycomb repressive complex 2 (PRC2), enforces transcriptional gene silencing across lineage-determining genomic loci. Gain-of-function somatic mutations at codon Y646 in EZH2 drive aberrant hypermethylation and transcriptional repression of tumor suppressor genes in diffuse large B-cell lymphoma and follicular lymphoma.
