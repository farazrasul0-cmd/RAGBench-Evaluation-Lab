Poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase (PARP) inhibitors exploit synthetic lethality in tumors exhibiting homologous recombination deficiency (HRD), particularly those harboring germline or somatic BRCA1 or BRCA2 mutations. Pharmacological trapping of PARP1 and PARP2 at sites of single-strand DNA breaks causes replication fork collapse into catastrophic double-strand breaks that cannot be faithfully repaired in homologous recombination-defective cells.
