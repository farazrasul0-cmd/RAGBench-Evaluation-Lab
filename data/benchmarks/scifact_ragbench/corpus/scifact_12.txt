Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) represents a next-generation antibody-drug conjugate comprising an anti-HER2 IgG1 antibody coupled via a cleavable tetrapeptide linker to a topoisomerase I inhibitor exatecan derivative payload. Demonstrating an elevated drug-to-antibody ratio (DAR) of approximately 8:1 and substantial membrane-permeable bystander antitumor effect, T-DXd demonstrates remarkable efficacy in HER2-low metastatic breast cancer.
