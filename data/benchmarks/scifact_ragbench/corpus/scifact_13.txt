Ferroptosis is an iron-dependent form of regulated non-apoptotic cell death characterized by the accumulation of lethal lipid reactive oxygen species (ROS) and lipid hydroperoxides. The system xc- cystine/glutamate antiporter and glutathione peroxidase 4 (GPX4) constitute the primary cellular defense axis against ferroptotic death. Pharmacological inactivation of GPX4 by RSL3 triggers catastrophic membrane lipid destruction.
