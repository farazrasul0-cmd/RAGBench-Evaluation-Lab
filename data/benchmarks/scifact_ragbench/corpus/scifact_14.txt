Personalized neoantigen vaccines leverage machine-learning algorithms and mass spectrometry immunopeptidomics to predict high-affinity patient-specific peptide binding to major histocompatibility complex (MHC) class I and class II molecules. Clinical trials evaluating synthetic long peptide and poly-epitope RNA vaccines demonstrate induction of potent neoantigen-specific neo-epitope polyfunctional CD4+ and CD8+ T-cell clones with long-term memory phenotypes.
