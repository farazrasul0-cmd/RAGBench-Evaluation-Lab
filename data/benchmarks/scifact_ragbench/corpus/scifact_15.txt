Mitochondrial fission and fusion govern metabolic plasticity, cellular bioenergetics, and reactive oxygen species homeostasis in proliferating stem cells and transformed malignancies. Dynamin-related protein 1 (DRP1) mediates mitochondrial fission, whereas mitofusins (MFN1 and MFN2) and optic atrophy 1 (OPA1) orchestrate outer and inner membrane fusion. Enhanced DRP1-mediated fragmentation promotes glycolytic reprogramming and metastatic invasion.
