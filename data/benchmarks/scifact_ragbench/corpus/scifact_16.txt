The cGAS-STING (cyclic GMP-AMP synthase - stimulator of interferon genes) cytosolic DNA sensing pathway detects aberrant cytosolic double-stranded DNA derived from chromosomal instability or viral infection. Upon binding 2'3'-cGAMP, STING translocates from the endoplasmic reticulum to the Golgi apparatus, recruiting TBK1 and activating IRF3 and NF-kB transcription factors to drive robust production of type I interferons.
