Proteolysis targeting chimeras (PROTACs) are heterobifunctional small molecules designed to selectively degrade target proteins of interest via the cellular ubiquitin-proteasome degradation machinery. A PROTAC consists of a target-binding moiety linked to an E3 ubiquitin ligase-recruiting ligand (such as CRBN or VHL). Inducing proximity leads to polyubiquitination of the target protein and subsequent degradation by the 26S proteasome.
