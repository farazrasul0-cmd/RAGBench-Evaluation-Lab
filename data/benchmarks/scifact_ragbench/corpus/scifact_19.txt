Macroautophagy is an evolutionary conserved lysosomal degradation pathway that degrades damaged organelles and misfolded protein aggregates during nutrient deprivation and metabolic stress. In established solid tumors, cytoprotective autophagy serves as a primary survival mechanism against cytotoxic chemotherapy and targeted agents. Pharmacological inhibition of autophagy with chloroquine or hydroxychloroquine restores chemosensitivity.
