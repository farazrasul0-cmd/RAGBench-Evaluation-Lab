Epstein-Barr virus (EBV) infects over 90% of the adult human population, establishing life-long asymptomatic latency in memory B lymphocytes. Expression of latent membrane proteins LMP1 and LMP2A mimics CD40 and B-cell receptor signaling, respectively. In immunocompromised settings, EBV drives lymphomagenesis including Burkitt lymphoma, Hodgkin lymphoma, and post-transplant lymphoproliferative disorders.
