Somatic gain-of-function mutations in Janus kinase 2 (JAK2 V617F) reside in the pseudokinase domain, disrupting auto-inhibitory constraints and constitutive STAT transcription factor phosphorylation. This hyperactive signaling drives erythrocytosis, leukocytosis, and thrombocytosis in polycythemia vera, essential thrombocythemia, and primary myelofibrosis, responsive to ruxolitinib inhibition.
