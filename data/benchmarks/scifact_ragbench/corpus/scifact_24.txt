Ligation of TNF receptor 1 (TNFR1) initiates assembly of signaling complex I, triggering NF-kB survival pathways. Under caspase-8 inhibition, deubiquitinated RIPK1 associates with RIPK3 to form the necrosome complex. RIPK3 phosphorylates the pseudokinase MLKL, driving MLKL oligomerization and plasma membrane translocation that causes necrotic membrane rupture and inflammatory DAMP release.
