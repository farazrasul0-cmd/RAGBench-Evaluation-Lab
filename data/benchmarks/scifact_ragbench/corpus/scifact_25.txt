Targeted alpha therapy using Actinium-225 labelled PSMA-617 ([225Ac]Ac-PSMA-617) delivers high-linear energy transfer (LET) alpha radiation specifically to prostate-specific membrane antigen expressing metastatic foci. Alpha particles deposit intense ionizing radiation over a short range of 40 to 100 micrometers, inducing lethal DNA double-strand cluster breaks with minimal myelosuppression.
