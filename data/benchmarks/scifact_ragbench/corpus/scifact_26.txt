The NLRP3 inflammasome is a tripartite cytosolic multiprotein complex comprising sensor NLRP3, adaptor ASC, and effector pro-caspase-1. Activation requires a two-step mechanism: transcriptional priming via NF-kB, followed by post-translational triggering via potassium efflux, lysosomal destabilization, or mitochondrial reactive oxygen species. Active caspase-1 cleaves pro-IL-1beta and gasdermin D to trigger pyroptosis.
