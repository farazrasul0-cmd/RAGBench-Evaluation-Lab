B-cell maturation antigen (BCMA) is preferentially expressed on late-stage memory B lymphocytes, plasmablasts, and malignant plasma cells, making it an ideal therapeutic target in relapsed multiple myeloma. Bispecific T-cell engager (BiTE) antibodies such as teclistamab cross-link BCMA on plasma cells with CD3 on cytotoxic T cells, inducing redirected lysis independent of MHC restriction.
