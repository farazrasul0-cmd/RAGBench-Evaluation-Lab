The PI3K-AKT-mTOR catalytic cascade coordinates glucose uptake, glycogen synthesis, and translational initiation downstream of the insulin receptor. Pharmacological pan-PI3K and alpha-isoform-specific inhibitors (alpelisib) acutely disrupt physiological insulin signaling in skeletal muscle and liver, prompting compensatory hyperinsulinemia and Grade 3/4 hyperglycemia in clinical trials.
