Clonal hematopoiesis of indeterminate potential (CHIP) occurs when somatic mutations in pre-leukemic driver genes (DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1) expand within hematopoietic stem and progenitor cell pools without cytopenia. Individuals harboring CHIP carry a 0.5% to 1.0% annual risk of developing hematologic neoplasms and a twofold elevated hazard ratio for premature atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease.
