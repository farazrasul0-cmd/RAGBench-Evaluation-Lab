BRCA1-associated protein 1 (BAP1) is a nuclear deubiquitinating enzyme that regulates chromatin remodeling, DNA double-strand break repair, and transcriptional elongation. Inactivating germline and somatic mutations in BAP1 predispose individuals to malignant mesothelioma, uveal melanoma, and renal cell carcinoma, sensitizing tumors to poly(ADP-ribose) polymerase and EZH2 inhibition.
