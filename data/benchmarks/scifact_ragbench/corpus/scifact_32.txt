Tumor-derived exosomes and microvesicles encapsulate non-coding RNAs, signaling lipids, and oncoproteins, transferring cargo horizontally to recipient stromal cells. Uptake of organotropic exosomal integrins (alpha6beta4 and alpha6beta1 for lung; alphavbeta5 for liver) primes regional vascular permeability, recruits bone marrow-derived myeloid cells, and establishes pre-metastatic microenvironments.
