To prevent on-target off-tumor cross-reactivity against healthy vital organs, synthetic biologists engineer split, universal, and programmable logic-gated CARs. AND-gate circuits require simultaneous co-recognition of dual antigens (e.g., HER2 and IL13Ralpha2) to elicit complete T-cell activation, whereas NOT-gate designs incorporate inhibitory receptors (iCARs) targeting self-antigens present exclusively on healthy tissues.
