Programmed death-ligand 1 (PD-L1) expression on tumor and myeloid cells is dynamically induced by interferon-gamma (IFN-gamma) secreted by activated cytotoxic T lymphocytes. IFN-gamma binding to IFNGR1/2 phosphorylates JAK1 and JAK2, leading to STAT1 homodimerization and transcriptional upregulation of interferon regulatory factor 1 (IRF1), which directly activates the CD274 promoter.
