CD47 functions as an innate immune checkpoint 'do not eat me' signal constitutively expressed on normal erythrocytes and upregulated across diverse hematologic and solid malignancies. Interaction of CD47 with signal regulatory protein alpha (SIRPalpha) on macrophages initiates immunoreceptor tyrosine-based inhibitory motif (ITIM) phosphorylation, recruiting SHP-1 to suppress non-muscle myosin IIA and abort phagocytic engulfment.
