Aberrant activation of the canonical Wnt/beta-catenin pathway, driven by activating CTNNB1 mutations or loss of APC/AXIN1, mediates primary resistance to immune checkpoint blockade in metastatic melanoma and hepatocellular carcinoma. Nuclear beta-catenin suppresses transcription of CCL4 chemokine, preventing recruitment of BATF3-lineage dendritic cells and causing a cold immune-excluded tumor phenotype.
