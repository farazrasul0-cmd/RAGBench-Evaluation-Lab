Disruption of the erythroid-specific GATA1-binding motif within the +58 kb enhancer of the BCL11A gene derepresses gamma-globin expression in human hematopoietic stem and progenitor cells. Ex vivo CRISPR-Cas9 ribonucleoprotein editing or adenine base editing of autologous CD34+ cells achieves sustained fetal hemoglobin (HbF) synthesis (>30%), preventing sickling and vaso-occlusive crises in clinical trials.
