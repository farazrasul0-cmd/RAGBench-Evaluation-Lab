Lutetium-177 ([177Lu]Lu-PSMA-617 and [177Lu]Lu-DOTATATE) emits beta particles with a mean tissue penetration path length of approximately 0.67 mm, minimizing collateral radiation damage to adjacent healthy parenchyma. In phase III clinical trials, [177Lu]Lu-PSMA-617 significantly extended overall survival and radiographic progression-free survival in patients with PSMA-positive metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer.
