The -124C>T and -146C>T somatic transitions in the hTERT core promoter create de novo consensus binding motifs (5'-CCGGAA-3') for the GA-binding protein transcription factor alpha subunit (GABPalpha). GABPalpha forms heterotetramers with GABPbeta, selectively binding mutant promoter configurations to drive high levels of telomerase expression across glioblastomas, melanomas, and bladder cancers.
