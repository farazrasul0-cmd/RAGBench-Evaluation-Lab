Tertiary lymphoid structures (TLS) are ectopic lymph node-like aggregates that develop within chronic inflammatory sites and tumor borders. Mature TLS containing CD20+ follicular B-cell zones, follicular dendritic cells, and germinal centers produce high-affinity tumor-antigen-specific IgG antibodies and somatic hypermutation, correlating with superior clinical responsiveness to PD-1/PD-L1 blockade.
