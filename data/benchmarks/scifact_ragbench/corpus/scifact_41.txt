The proto-oncogene MYC orchestrates global transcriptional amplification across thousands of metabolic genes, upregulating glutaminase (GLS) and the SLC1A5 glutamine transporter. MYC-driven lymphomas become critically addicted to exogenous glutamine influx to replenish tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle intermediates via anaplerosis, conferring exquisite sensitivity to small-molecule GLS inhibitors (CB-839).
