Chromosomal translocations involving the mixed lineage leukemia 1 (MLL1/KMT2A) gene generate oncogenic fusion proteins that require the nuclear scaffold protein menin to associate with chromatin. Small-molecule menin-MLL inhibitors (revumenib and ziftomenib) displace menin, repressing leukemogenic target genes MEIS1 and HOXA9, and inducing robust hematopoietic differentiation and clinical remissions.
