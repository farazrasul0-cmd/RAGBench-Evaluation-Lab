The CpG island methylator phenotype (CIMP) in colorectal cancer is characterized by widespread epigenetic promoter hypermethylation of MLH1, CDKN2A, and other key tumor suppressor genes, frequently co-occurring with the BRAF V600E mutation. Longitudinal multi-region bisulfite sequencing demonstrates that epigenetic epimutations exhibit distinct branching evolution patterns concordant with somatic clonal expansions.
