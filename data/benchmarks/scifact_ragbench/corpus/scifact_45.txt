Aberrant activation of the classical complement cascade mediates inappropriate microglial synaptic engulfment in Alzheimer's disease models. Oligomeric amyloid-beta (Abeta) and hyperphosphorylated tau provoke deposition of complement proteins C1q and C3 onto vulnerable synaptic terminals, marking synapses for CR3-dependent phagocytic elimination by activated microglia prior to overt neuronal loss.
