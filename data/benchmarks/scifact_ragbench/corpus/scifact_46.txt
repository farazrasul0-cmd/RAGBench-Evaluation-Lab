Molecular glue degraders alter the surface topography of E3 ubiquitin ligase complexes to create neo-substrates that would not otherwise interact with the ligase. Thalidomide, lenalidomide, and CC-92480 (mezigdomide) bind cereblon (CRBN), recruiting zinc-finger transcription factors IKZF1 (Ikaros) and IKZF3 (Aiolos) for ubiquitination and rapid proteasomal degradation in multiple myeloma.
