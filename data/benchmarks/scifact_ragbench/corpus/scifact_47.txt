Inactivation of the von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) tumor suppressor gene prevents prolyl-hydroxylated HIF-1alpha and HIF-2alpha from being recognized and targeted for proteasomal degradation by the VHL-elongin B/C-cullin 2 complex. Unchecked accumulation of HIF-2alpha heterodimerizes with HIF-1beta, driving transcription of VEGF, PDGF, and GLUT1 in clear cell renal cell carcinoma, successfully inhibited by belzutifan.
