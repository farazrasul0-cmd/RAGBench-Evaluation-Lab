N6-methyladenosine (m6A) is the most abundant internal mRNA chemical modification in eukaryotic transcriptomes, catalyzed by the METTL3-METTL14-WTAP methyltransferase complex and reversibly removed by FTO and ALKBH5 erasers. Cytoplasmic YTHDF reader proteins recognize m6A-modified transcripts to accelerate mRNA decay or enhance cap-independent translation in glioblastoma stem cells.
