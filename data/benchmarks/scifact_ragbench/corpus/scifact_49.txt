Commensal gut anaerobic Clostridia ferment dietary soluble fiber into short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs), predominantly butyrate, propionate, and acetate. Butyrate acts as a natural histone deacetylase (HDAC) inhibitor, enhancing histone H3 acetylation at the Foxp3 promoter and conserved non-coding sequence 1 (CNS1), directly inducing anti-inflammatory colonic regulatory T (Treg) cell differentiation.
