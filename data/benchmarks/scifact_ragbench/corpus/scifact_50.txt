Blinatumomab is a recombinant tandem single-chain variable fragment (scFv) antibody construct that physically connects CD19 on normal and malignant B cells to CD3 on T lymphocytes. Establishing an immunological synapse initiates polyclonal T-cell activation, perforin and granzyme secretion, and target B-cell apoptosis with sub-picomolar potency, achieving minimal residual disease negativity in precursor B-cell ALL.
